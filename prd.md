INTRODUCING BU-KEKS.
1.	PRODUCT OVERVIEW: It is a campus web application that connects babcock university students with tricycle ( keke) drivers on campus so that students can easily see available keke’s and book a ride and drivers can see where students are waiting to aid in better movement around campus

2.	Problem statement: Students and even staffs rely heavily on Tricycle (Keke) to move around campus, however there is a limited number of keke's combined with uncoordinated dispatch which leads to students being delayed and drivers waste time circling low demand zones and zero visibility for those going to more distant places e.g Buth, pioneer youth church.

3.	Business objectives: The main aim of this idea/business is to link students to Keke drivers, for easier access and to eliminate delays and for those going to distant places, we want to provide more visibility.

4.	Target users: students and even lecturers, basically anyone who wants to move around campus with the aid of keke's

5.	Stakeholders: Babcock university student association.

6.	User stories: To understand how this affects both sides daily, our group observed campus transport and interviewed 17 Students (freshers and returning students) and 6 Keke drivers.
What Students Currently Do
Struggle to get Kekes for 7:00 AM classes: Most students we spoke with said getting a Keke on time for
7: 00 AM classes are a right pain as they're forced to leave their hostels early and just hope an empty Keke gets by.
Walk around and wait at junctions: Students walk from spot to spot looking for Kekes or stand at busy
points whilst the already full Kekes go by, particularly when you're going a long way across campus. Ask other students where to find Kekes: Freshers especially do not know the normal spots where Kekes pass or park so they have to ask the older students where to catch one.
Group ride mismatches: When a group of 4 or 5 friends are going to class together, a smaller 3-seater Keke will often stop for them but has to turn them down as it can only take 3 passengers.

7.	Functional requirements: To bridge the gap between limited tricycle (Keke) and the high student demand we propose 'BABCOCK WHEELS', a web application that enables communication between students and Keke divers; It helps with matching students heading in the same direction especially far distances, maximizing seat utilization per trip and effectively increasing campus transit capacity without needing more physical vehicles.

8.	Non- functional requirements:
. Pick campus locations, group size, and Keke type: A student selects a campus pickup spot, chooses their destination, selects how many students are riding (1 to 5), and picks
Whether they need a 3 seater or a 5 seater Keke or whether they opt to pay for all 3 or all 5 seats for a quicker direct trip.
. See nearby Kekes and driver willingness: The app uses predefined campus zones/distances between
landmarks so students can see which Kekes are close by, and drivers see the destination, passenger count,
and full-Keke payment option prior to acceptance.
. Accept and meet: A driver with matching seat capacity accepts the request if both sides agree, and either
•	either they drive to the pickup point or the student comes and meets them there so they can be off straight

9.	  Scope and priorities: student authentication and verification, various campus landmarks, keke types or sitting capacity( 3-5 seater).

10.	Acceptance criteria:  For acceptance into the platform students will be able to login using their matric no, GPS tracking in the first version to enable both students and drivers know how far apart from each other they are drivers as well will have their own accounts.

11.	Assumptions and other questions: Out of Scope for Version 1: In-app wallet/card payments (cash or transfer remains), live turn-by-turn map navigation and app store downloads (runs in a phone browser).

12.	First Version (MVP) Features
Version 1 focuses strictly on solving the core problem cleanly before choosing any programming language or framework
Student and Driver Login: Separate accounts for students and verified campus Keke drivers (registered as  either a 3 seater or a 5 seater Keke).
Campus Stops and Distance Zones: Predefined list of campus pickup and drop-off points (SAT, BUSA, BBS, hostels, gates) With simple zone distance indicators so the students know how close a Keke is.
Seat Count and Full-Keke Option: Lets drivers see exact passenger counts beforehand (e.g., 5 students going to class) and lets a student choose to pay for all 3 or 5 seats if both parties agree so the driver can set off immediately.
